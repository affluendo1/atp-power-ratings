MODEL DOCUMENTATION

Methodology

Every rating comes from the published ATP-only ledger and a reproducible scoreline model.

ATP V1

  1. Opponent-adjusted game-score likelihood: p = logistic((θᵢ − θⱼ) / 0.75).
  2. Recent results matter more: a 300-day half-life within a rolling three-year window.
  3. Best-of-five conventional-set games count, but every match is capped at 28 game-equivalents.
  4. Match tiebreak points are not treated as games; retirements and walkovers remain visible but do not fit ratings.
  5. Power is shown as 1500 + 600θ; the 95% range comes from the centred Laplace uncertainty estimate.
